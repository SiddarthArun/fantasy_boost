How It Works

Fantasy BOOST pulls three seasons of NFL play-by-play data via nflreadpy, engineering per-position features including exponentially weighted moving averages (EWMA) of targets, touches, and Expected Points Added (EPA), weighted opportunity metrics, prior-season form, and rolling defensive allowances. It trains per-position XGBRegressor models (WR, RB, QB, TE) to predict PPR fantasy points for the upcoming week.

Known Limitations

  • Special teams (defenses and kickers) are excluded, and flex rankings are not automatically computed.
  • Quarterback performance inherently exhibits higher volatility and lower predictability than skill positions.
  • Real-time injury status is not automatically factored in real-time, though players with sustained volume are prioritized.
  • Preseason projections lack historical rookie/offseason data until regular season stats accumulate.
